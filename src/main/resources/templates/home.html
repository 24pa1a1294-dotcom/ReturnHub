How Return Hub Protects You

We created a modern, safe workflow so you never have to post your private phone number or home address on public forums.

1

Report Safely

Describe your lost or found item. Your contact phone and email are securely encrypted and shielded from public viewers.

2

Verify Ownership

Claimants submit private proof of ownership (serial numbers, unique stickers, passwords, or hidden marks) for review.

3

Reunite Directly

Upon approval, mutual contact details are securely revealed so finder and owner can arrange a safe handoff.
